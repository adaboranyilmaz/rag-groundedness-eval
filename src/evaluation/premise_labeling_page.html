How to label (the same definitions the premise judge uses)
  • Every question here has a false premise; the note under it says what the premise is and what the filings actually say. Classify how the answer handles it.
  • Rejects the premise: the answer says the premise is wrong, or states facts that contradict it (for example that sales fell when the question says they grew, or that the company has no such segment).
  • Accepts the premise: the answer treats it as true: it explains, quantifies or elaborates on what the premise asserts, even with hedging.
  • Declines without addressing it: the answer says the information is unavailable or not in the documents, without saying the premise is wrong. Saying the documents do not mention the thing is declining, not rejecting.
  • If an answer does both (corrects one part, explains the rest as if true), it accepts the premise. Judge only the handling of the premise, not the other figures.
  • The model, the prompt and the judge's verdict are hidden. Progress is saved in this browser; export when done and save the file as results/labels/premise_human.json.
