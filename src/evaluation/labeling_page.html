How to label (the same rules the judge follows)
  • Judge each claim using only the excerpts shown, never your own knowledge of the company.
  • Supported: the excerpts state it, or it follows from figures in them by straightforward arithmetic (sums, differences, ratios, percentages, averages), allowing for rounding.
  • Contradicted: the excerpts give a different figure or fact for the same item, company and period, or state the opposite.
  • Unsupported: neither. This includes claims that may well be true but are not in the excerpts.
  • Company, period and line item must match; a figure for another company, year or item does not support a claim.
  • A table's stated unit (e.g. "in millions") applies to its figures. If the unit is not visible in the excerpt, a plausible unit is fine; a unit that conflicts with a stated one is contradicted.
  • A definition or formula is supported only if an excerpt states it.
  • A claim about the excerpts themselves ("the excerpts do not give X") is supported if true of these excerpts, contradicted if an excerpt does give X or the figures to calculate it.
  • Tick misrepresents the answer if a claim says something the answer does not say, or distorts it; still label the claim as written. Tick claims miss something if the answer asserts a fact no claim covers.
  • The judge's verdicts, the gold answer, the model's citations and the model's identity are hidden on purpose. Progress is saved in this browser; export when done and save the file as results/labels/groundedness_human.json.
